Our little dream

A story to slow down and share

Little Dream Tales is a Facebook page featuring bedtime animation videos for children ages 3–10. Each story brings together gentle narration, soft music, and friendly characters in a calm, family-friendly setting.

Our stories are made to be enjoyed together. Every child winds down in their own way, so we hope these tales can be one small, comforting part of your family’s bedtime routine.
