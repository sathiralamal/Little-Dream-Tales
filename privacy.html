The important grown-up page

Your family’s privacy matters.

Here’s what to know about privacy when you visit the Little Dream Tales website.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Information on this website

This is a simple informational website. It does not provide account registration, contact forms, or a way to submit personal information. We do not use this website to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally set cookies or use analytics tools. Your web hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as server logs, to deliver and protect the website. For details about that processing, consult the provider’s privacy information.

Facebook and other external links

Links on this website take you to Facebook, where Little Dream Tales shares its videos. If you follow a link, Facebook may collect or use information according to its own privacy policy and settings. This website does not control Facebook’s services or privacy practices.

Our Facebook page is facebook.com/littledreamtales27.

Children and family use

Our stories are intended for children to enjoy with a parent or caregiver. This website is for informational purposes and does not ask children to provide personal information.

Changes and questions

We may update this page if the website or its practices change. Please check here for the latest version. For questions about the Facebook page or its content, visit us on Facebook.
